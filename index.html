The Venue

Strömfors is located in the eastern part of Uusimaa, approximately an hour’s drive from central Helsinki or the Helsinki-Vantaa Airport via the E18 motorway. The nearest town, Loviisa, is 20 minutes away; you can reach Kotka in half an hour. If it’s your first time in Finland, Porvoo is also a charming old town on the way. There is plenty of parking around the Ruukki village, including space for RVs.

Using public transport is possible when travelling from Helsinki or Kotka. The long-distance coaches from Kamppi in central Helsinki operate hourly with a journey time of around an hour and a half; we recommend booking in advance via the Matkahuolto or OnniBus website. The nearest stop is called Ruotsinpyhtää Liittymä 68 (Ruotsinpyhtää Junction 68) and is located 5 kilometers from our venue - as such, we will offer a ride for this leg for those who need it. The local taxi for Ruotsinpyhtää and Loviisa is Taksikuljetus Hill, who also accept advance reservations. We will offer a group charter back to Helsinki late on Saturday night for those who require it.

Acommodation

The Iron Works’ hotel Bed & Bistro is made up of the main manor, dating to 1892, and the single-story Krouvinmäki inn, completed in 1784. The 16 rooms in the accommodation range in size from 1-5 person occupancy. The area is also home to an additional guest house, and wilderness accommodation via Wellsters.

Bed & Bistro have kindly held rooms with priority for our wedding party for both Friday the 24th and Saturday the 25th of July; as such, you will not see these listed on the conventional online booking calendar. If you would like to stay at Bed & Bistro, please email front desk at info@bedandbistro.fi and mention Eve + Conor in your message or subject line. We recommend booking at least three months before the wedding date, as this is when rooms will be released to a general audience. Find pricing here.

The Strömfors website offers further information on activities in the area; in addition to quaint museums and shops, the canoeing routes and Kukuljärvi lake hike is recommended. We have also reserved the lakefront sauna for our party’s use on Sunday the 26th!

The nearby Aeronautica centre offers indoor surfing and a skydive at Europe’s tallest wind tunnel, and the Visit Kotka-Hamina site is full of suggestions and additional hotel or cabin options if you’d like to extend your visit to a weekend away. However, the Kotka Maritime Festival (Meripäivät) lands on the same weekend, which may fill accommodation faster than usual.
